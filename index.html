Mental Preparation

Visualization (10-15 min)

Before bed, visualize your race:

  • See yourself running strong and confident
  • Visualize smooth acceleration and finishing kick
  • Picture crossing the finish line with great effort
  • Feel the emotions of a successful race

Positive Affirmations

Tell yourself:

  • "I am prepared and ready"
  • "I will run my race with confidence"
  • "My training has prepared me well"
  • "I belong on that track"

Sleep & Mindset

  • Get to bed early (10-11pm)
  • Avoid excessive screen time 1 hour before bed
  • Focus on the process, not the outcome
  • Accept that race day nerves are normal and positive

Mental Preparation

Pre-Race Breathing (5 min)

Calm your nerves with breathing work:

  • Box breathing: Inhale 4 counts, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4 (repeat 5-10x)
  • Focus on steady, controlled breathing
  • Feel your body relax with each breath

Race Strategy & Focus

  • Remember your race plan (pacing, splits, kick timing)
  • Know your competitors and what to expect
  • Focus on YOUR race - run your own race
  • Break the race into segments (easier to manage)

Confidence Trigger Statements

Repeat during warmup:

  • "I've trained for this"
  • "I am strong and fast"
  • "I will execute my race plan"
  • "Let's go racing"

Minute by Minute Before Gun

  • 10 min before: Final visualization - see a strong race
  • 5 min before: Get to starting line, focus inward
  • 2 min before: Get in blocks/ready position, breathe
  • At the gun: Trust your training, execute, RACE!
