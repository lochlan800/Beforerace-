🧠 Mental Preparation

Pick and choose the techniques that work best for you today.

🎵 Calming Music for Mental Prep

Start with calming instrumental music to support your breathing and tapping work:

  • Spotify Playlists: Search for "Pre-Race Calm", "Meditation Flow", or "Lo-Fi Focus"
  • YouTube Channels: "Peaceful Piano", "Ambient Study Music", "Zen Music"
  • Duration: 10-20 minutes of continuous play
  • Key: Instrumental, no lyrics, steady rhythm (60-80 BPM works best)

🫀 Bilateral EFT Tapping with Breathing

Duration: 5-10 minutes

Setup: Play calming music, find a quiet space

  • Rhythm: Tap alternating sides of your body in a steady beat (left-right-left-right)
  • Timing: Tap approximately every 1 second (or follow the music beat)
  • Tapping Points:
    • Alternate between left and right shoulders
    • Or tap left and right knees/legs alternately
    • Or alternate tapping sides of your chest
  • Breathing: Inhale for 4 counts (4 taps) → Exhale for 4 counts (4 taps)
  • Focus: Feel calm, safe, and ready. Let go of tension with each exhale

🎯 Body Scan Exercise

Duration: 5-7 minutes

Systematically check in with your body and release tension:

  • Head & Face: Notice any tension, relax your jaw, soften your forehead
  • Neck & Shoulders: Feel where you're holding stress, let it drop on an exhale
  • Chest & Heart: Feel your heartbeat, breathe into any tightness
  • Arms & Hands: Shake them out gently, feel them becoming light and strong
  • Core & Legs: Feel the strength in your legs, ready to run with power
  • Feet: Feel them grounded and connected to the track
  • Close: Take 3 deep breaths and feel fully present in your body

🌍 Grounding Exercise (5 Senses)

Duration: 3-5 minutes

Anchor yourself to the present moment:

  • See (5 things): Name 5 things you can see around you in detail
  • Touch (4 things): Notice 4 textures you can feel - skin, clothing, ground
  • Hear (3 things): Identify 3 sounds - music, birds, heartbeat
  • Smell (2 things): Notice 2 scents - grass, air, your skin
  • Taste (1 thing): Feel 1 taste - water, your mouth, fresh air
  • Result: You're now fully present and connected to your surroundings

💪 Confidence & Power Pose

Duration: 2-3 minutes

Use your body to build mental confidence:

  • Power Stance: Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, hands on hips or arms raised
  • Chest Expansion: Open your chest, shoulders back, head up
  • Hold: Stay in this position for 2-3 minutes while breathing deeply
  • Mental State: Tell yourself: "I am strong. I am fast. I am ready."
  • Feel: Notice the confidence building in your body
  • Alternative: Light shadow running - take a few powerful strides with confident form

🏁 Race Visualization

Duration: 5-10 minutes

Mental rehearsal of your perfect race:

  • The Moment Before: Feel yourself calm and ready at the start line
  • The Gun Fires: Visualize explosive, confident start - smooth and powerful
  • Early Race (First 200m): See yourself in position, relaxed, on pace
  • Middle Section: Picture yourself running with confidence and control
  • The Final Kick: See your legs responding, accelerating past competitors
  • The Finish: Visualize crossing the finish line strong, with great effort
  • The Feeling: Feel the emotions of a successful, powerful race
  • Open Your Eyes: Take 3 deep breaths and feel ready to execute

✨ Final Confidence Statements

Repeat these before you get to the starting line:

  • "I've trained for this moment"
  • "My body is strong and ready"
  • "I will run my race with confidence"
  • "I belong on this track"
  • "Let's go racing" 🏃

🏆 Post-Race Recovery

You ran a great race! Now focus on recovery to come back stronger.

⏱️ First 30 Minutes Post-Race

Immediate Recovery Priority

  • Cool Down Walk: Easy walking for 5-10 minutes to bring heart rate down gradually
  • Hydration: Drink water or electrolyte drink immediately - sip steadily
  • Quick Carbs: Eat something light with carbs within 30 min (banana, energy bar, dates)
  • Celebrate: You earned it! Acknowledge your effort and what you accomplished

🥤 Nutrition & Hydration

Recovery Window (30 min - 2 hours post-race)

  • Carbs + Protein Meal: Eat within 1-2 hours (3:1 carbs to protein ratio is ideal)
  • Examples: Pasta with chicken, rice with fish, bagel with turkey, eggs on toast
  • Hydration: Drink 150% of body weight lost in sweat over 4-6 hours
  • Electrolytes: Include sodium in recovery drink or meal to aid hydration retention
  • Throughout the Day: Continue hydrating - drink more than usual
  • Avoid: Excessive caffeine, alcohol (delays recovery)

❄️ Cold Therapy (Ice Bath/Cold Water)

Timing: 30 min - 2 hours after finishing race

Duration: 10-15 minutes (can break into 2x 7-8 min sessions)

  • Temperature: 50-59°F (10-15°C) is ideal - cold but tolerable
  • Submersion: Submerge legs and lower body if full bath not available
  • Breathing: Use controlled breathing to manage cold shock
  • Benefits: Reduces inflammation, speeds recovery, decreases muscle soreness
  • Alternative: Cold shower (1-2 min) if ice bath unavailable
  • Post-Bath: Dry off, warm up, drink something warm

🧘 Stretching & Mobility Work

Timing: Start gently 30-60 min post-race

Duration: 15-20 minutes

  • Static Stretches (Hold 30-60 sec each):
    • Quads (standing or lying)
    • Hamstrings (forward fold or seated)
    • Calves (wall stretch)
    • Hip flexors (lunge position)
    • Glutes (figure 4 stretch)
    • Lower back (lying knee hug)
  • Key: Gentle stretches - don't push hard on tired muscles
  • Breathing: Deep, relaxed breathing into each stretch

🔄 Foam Rolling & Self-Massage

Timing: 1-2 hours post-race or next day (not immediately)

Duration: 10-15 minutes

  • Foam Roll (gentle pressure):
    • Quads (slow passes, 1-2 min each leg)
    • IT band (side-lying, slow roll)
    • Hamstrings (seated, slow roll)
    • Calves (both legs together)
  • Massage Ball Work: Target tight spots, hold and breathe for 30 sec
  • Manual Massage: Use hands to massage and release tense areas
  • Key: Roll slowly - speed doesn't help recovery

🔋 Active Recovery

Day After Race (24 hours post-race)

  • Easy Walk: 20-30 min easy walk - gets blood flowing without stress
  • Gentle Swimming: Light swimming can aid recovery (if available)
  • Yoga or Tai Chi: Gentle, relaxing movement
  • Avoid: Any hard running or intense workouts for at least 2-3 days
  • Goal: Promote blood flow without adding stress to body

😴 Sleep & Rest

  • Priority: Sleep is when your body repairs and adapts
  • That Night: Aim for extra sleep (9-10 hours if possible)
  • Following Days: Prioritize sleep - get 7-9+ hours for next 2-3 days
  • Naps: 20-30 min power naps are helpful for recovery
  • Environment: Cool, dark, quiet room for best sleep quality
  • Relaxation: Take it easy - minimal physical and mental stress

🧠 Mental Recovery & Reflection

  • Celebrate: You completed your race! Acknowledge your effort
  • Reflect: What went well? What would you do differently?
  • Journal: Write down how you felt, what you learned, race experience
  • Stay Positive: Be kind to yourself about race performance
  • Mental Rest: Give your mind time to recover too - reduce stressful activities
  • Connect: Share your race story with friends/family
