Visualization (10-15 min)

Before bed, visualize your race:

  • See yourself running strong and confident
  • Visualize smooth acceleration and finishing kick
  • Picture crossing the finish line with great effort
  • Feel the emotions of a successful race

Sleep & Mindset

  • Get to bed early (10-11pm)
  • Avoid excessive screen time 1 hour before bed
  • Focus on the process, not the outcome
  • Accept that race day nerves are normal and positive

🧠 Mental Preparation

Pick and choose the techniques that work best for you today.

🎵 Calming Music for Mental Prep

Start with calming instrumental music to support your breathing and tapping work:

  • Spotify Playlists: Search for "Pre-Race Calm", "Meditation Flow", or "Lo-Fi Focus"
  • YouTube Channels: "Peaceful Piano", "Ambient Study Music", "Zen Music"
  • Duration: 10-20 minutes of continuous play
  • Key: Instrumental, no lyrics, steady rhythm (60-80 BPM works best)

🫀 Bilateral EFT Tapping with Breathing

Duration: 5-10 minutes

Setup: Play calming music, find a quiet space

  • Rhythm: Tap alternating sides of your body in a steady beat (left-right-left-right)
  • Timing: Tap approximately every 1 second (or follow the music beat)
  • Tapping Points:
    • Alternate between left and right shoulders
    • Or tap left and right knees/legs alternately
    • Or alternate tapping sides of your chest
  • Breathing: Inhale for 4 counts (4 taps) → Exhale for 4 counts (4 taps)
  • Focus: Feel calm, safe, and ready. Let go of tension with each exhale

🎯 Body Scan Exercise

Duration: 5-7 minutes

Systematically check in with your body and release tension:

  • Head & Face: Notice any tension, relax your jaw, soften your forehead
  • Neck & Shoulders: Feel where you're holding stress, let it drop on an exhale
  • Chest & Heart: Feel your heartbeat, breathe into any tightness
  • Arms & Hands: Shake them out gently, feel them becoming light and strong
  • Core & Legs: Feel the strength in your legs, ready to run with power
  • Feet: Feel them grounded and connected to the track
  • Close: Take 3 deep breaths and feel fully present in your body

🌍 Grounding Exercise (5 Senses)

Duration: 3-5 minutes

Anchor yourself to the present moment:

  • See (5 things): Name 5 things you can see around you in detail
  • Touch (4 things): Notice 4 textures you can feel - skin, clothing, ground
  • Hear (3 things): Identify 3 sounds - music, birds, heartbeat
  • Smell (2 things): Notice 2 scents - grass, air, your skin
  • Taste (1 thing): Feel 1 taste - water, your mouth, fresh air
  • Result: You're now fully present and connected to your surroundings

💪 Confidence & Power Pose

Duration: 2-3 minutes

Use your body to build mental confidence:

  • Power Stance: Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, hands on hips or arms raised
  • Chest Expansion: Open your chest, shoulders back, head up
  • Hold: Stay in this position for 2-3 minutes while breathing deeply
  • Mental State: Tell yourself: "I am strong. I am fast. I am ready."
  • Feel: Notice the confidence building in your body
  • Alternative: Light shadow running - take a few powerful strides with confident form

🏁 Race Visualization

Duration: 5-10 minutes

Mental rehearsal of your perfect race:

  • The Moment Before: Feel yourself calm and ready at the start line
  • The Gun Fires: Visualize explosive, confident start - smooth and powerful
  • Early Race (First 200m): See yourself in position, relaxed, on pace
  • Middle Section: Picture yourself running with confidence and control
  • The Final Kick: See your legs responding, accelerating past competitors
  • The Finish: Visualize crossing the finish line strong, with great effort
  • The Feeling: Feel the emotions of a successful, powerful race
  • Open Your Eyes: Take 3 deep breaths and feel ready to execute

✨ Final Confidence Statements

Repeat these before you get to the starting line:

  • "I've trained for this moment"
  • "My body is strong and ready"
  • "I will run my race with confidence"
  • "I belong on this track"
  • "Let's go racing" 🏃
